- **Deletions may be broad, not granular.** We may delete an entire profile's associated data rather than individual capsules. Item-by-item selective deletion is not guaranteed.
- **We cannot read capsule content before deleting it.** Capsules are encrypted on your device. We operate on encrypted blobs we cannot inspect. You can verify this via our open-source code.
- **Deletion actions on Pinata may be anonymous and irreversible.** We cannot undo a deletion once performed.
- **Deletion from our nodes does not delete from IPFS.** If other nodes have pinned your content, it persists on the decentralized network beyond our control.
- **We have no liability for any deletion**, whether accidental, intentional, or due to any cause.

### 6.2 Immutable Blockchain Data

The following is permanently stored on Sepolia blockchain and cannot be deleted by anyone, including us: wallet addresses, transaction hashes and timestamps, gas fees, capsule creation proofs, and all smart contract interaction records.
